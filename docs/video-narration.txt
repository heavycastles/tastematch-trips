[0:00–0:20 Hook]
Three friends, one trip. Aziz loves Spirited Away and Patagonia. Malika loves Daft Punk and Wes Anderson. Rustam loves Anthony Bourdain and Chef's Table. Classic planners pick a city for the average — and someone loses. This is TasteMatch Trips: a fairness-aware group-travel agent on Qloo Taste AI.

[0:20–0:45 Problem]
Taste is cultural, not just a survey score. We ask only nicknames and cultural favourites — no personal data. The agent resolves each favourite in Qloo, scores destinations for the group and for every traveler, then ranks by a fairness score: forty percent mean affinity, forty percent minimum, twenty percent group affinity. The least-matched traveler is named out loud.

[0:45–1:25 Live plan]
We load the sample group and hit Plan. Against the live hackathon API, all six favourites resolve. No guesses, nothing unmatched. Top destination: Sayulita, Nayarit, group fit seventy-eight point nine, least-matched traveler Aziz. Then Santa Fe and Ojai. Sayulita wins not because it is hottest for any one person, but because nobody is left behind.

[1:25–2:00 Itinerary]
The itinerary is round-robin so the fair share is two slots each. Day one morning: Hacienda Camino al Mar for Aziz, affinity point nine two, driven by Spirited Away and Patagonia. Afternoon: Playa Sayulita for Malika. Evening: Mita Mary for Rustam's food tastes. Every pick cites the Qloo affinity and the favourites it came from.

[2:00–2:25 Trace]
The agent trace shows every Qloo call: six searches, four destination insight calls, three place calls. Responses stay in memory only. Results are aggregate taste affinities — not predictions about any individual. Nicknames only.
